## Professional Summary

Cell and developmental biologist with a PhD and over a decade of research experience at UC Riverside and UCLA, with international exposure at UGhent (Belgium) and FAFU (China). Track record of first-author and co-author publications in high-impact journals (*Nature Communications*, *Cell*, *The Plant Cell*). Combines deep scientific rigor with expertise in quantitative imaging (FLIM, AFM, confocal), molecular biology, and data science (Python, R) with strategic expertise in R&D project management, technology transfer, intellectual property, and entrepreneurship. Experienced in securing and/or managing competitive grants (NIH, NSF, FONDECYT, FONDEF). Fluent English and Spanish; international professional network across three continents.
